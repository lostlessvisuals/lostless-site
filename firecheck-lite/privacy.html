FIRECHECK LITE

Privacy Policy

FireCheck Lite is designed to keep extinguisher inspection records local to your iPhone. This policy reflects the current release build.

Data we collect

FireCheck Lite does not require account creation and does not collect personal account data through the app.

Inspection data

  • Properties, extinguisher records, inspections, notes, and export history are stored locally on-device.
  • The app does not upload inspection data to a remote service.
  • Generated PDF and CSV files are created only when the user explicitly requests them.

Camera, NFC, and photos

  • Camera access is optional and is only used for QR scanning and optional inspection photos.
  • NFC access is optional and only used to read compatible tags when available.
  • If these permissions are denied, the app still supports manual entry workflows.

Analytics and tracking

The current release does not include third-party analytics SDKs, ad tracking, or remote profiling.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock handled through Apple’s StoreKit purchase and restore flow. The app uses network access only for those purchase-related workflows.
