NO VACANCY

Privacy Policy

No Vacancy is designed to keep focus-blocking data on your iPhone. This policy reflects the current release build.

Data we store

  • Presets for selected apps, websites, and categories.
  • Schedules, daily limits, session logs, and reflections.
  • Local app settings, including an optional hashed PIN if enabled.

Data we collect

No Vacancy does not require account creation and does not collect personal data on an external server.

Screen Time permissions

No Vacancy requests Screen Time-related permissions only so it can apply and manage the blocking rules you configure. If permission is denied or revoked, the app can still open, but blocking cannot be enforced.

Notifications

If enabled, notifications are used only for local reminders and status prompts on the device.

Analytics and tracking

The current release does not include third-party analytics SDKs, ad tracking, or server-side profiling.

Purchases

No Vacancy Pro is a one-time unlock handled through Apple's StoreKit purchase and restore flow. Purchase receipts are handled by Apple, not by a separate No Vacancy backend.

Contact

If you need help with privacy or support questions, use lostless.live/contact/.
