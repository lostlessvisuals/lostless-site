ai pipeline consulting

I help creative teams move from AI experiments to usable production systems.

Through short R&D sprints, workflow audits, ComfyUI builds, TouchDesigner prototypes and team training, I help studios test where generative AI actually belongs in their pipeline.

Available for select advisory, R&D and workflow design engagements with VFX, commercial, experiential and in-house creative teams.

services

where the work usually lands

AI pipeline advisory

For studios and creative teams exploring where generative AI fits into production workflows. I help teams evaluate current tools, identify practical use cases and separate useful workflows from demos that will not survive production.

Workflow prototyping

Short R&D engagements focused on building repeatable visual workflows using tools like ComfyUI, image-to-video, video-to-video, cleanup, looping, upscaling and real-time playback systems.

Team enablement

Workshops and internal sessions for artists, producers and creative leads who need a practical understanding of how AI can be used responsibly in visual production.

Real-time & experiential systems

AI-assisted visual systems for live performance, installations, immersive screens and TouchDesigner-driven environments.
