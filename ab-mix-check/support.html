AB MIX CHECK

Support

AB Mix Check is a local, headphone-first A/B reference tool for iPhone. This page covers the core workflow, expected behavior, and basic troubleshooting for release support.

Core workflow

  • Import two local audio files in the Library.
  • Tap Start A/B Check to open the player.
  • Switch between Mix and Reference with loudness matching enabled when making balance decisions.
  • Use markers and notes to capture the exact place you want to revisit.
  • Export TXT or CSV if you want a handoff to Files or Notes.

What is expected

  • All processing stays on-device.
  • No account is required.
  • Mono, Mid, and Side audibly change playback in Critical Listening.
  • Blind Compare hides source labels until you reveal the mapping.

Troubleshooting

  • If playback is silent, reopen the session and tap Play again after switching sources.
  • If an imported file becomes unavailable, re-import it from the Library.
  • If export does not appear, tap Export TXT or Export CSV again and use the iOS share sheet.
  • If a comparison feels unfair, turn on Match Reference To Mix.
