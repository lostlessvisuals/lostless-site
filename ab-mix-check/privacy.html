AB MIX CHECK

Privacy Policy

AB Mix Check is designed to keep audio-comparison work local to your iPhone. This policy reflects the current release build.

Data we collect

AB Mix Check does not require account creation and does not collect personal account data through the app.

Audio files and session data

  • Imported audio files remain local to the device and under the user’s control.
  • Markers, notes, loop points, and session summaries are stored locally on-device.
  • The app does not upload imported audio or session notes to a remote server.

Analytics and tracking

The current release does not include third-party analytics SDKs, ad tracking, or server-side profiling.

Exports and sharing

TXT and CSV exports are only created when the user explicitly taps an export action. Shared files leave the app only through the standard iOS share sheet selected by the user.

Purchases

The current release hides the Pro purchase UI while the App Store product is not live. No in-app purchase flow is exposed in this build.

Contact

If you need to reach the publisher about privacy or support, use the contact details on lostless.live/contact/.
