making ion storm in overhead

Ion Storm is a live visual I made in Overhead with an agent. Fine indigo strands gather into ridges and drift apart. They keep moving through the quiet stretches of a track, with white lightning crossing the field when the drums land.

getting the flash right

The agent built the lightning effect from scratch. Its first pass kept bending after the hit. I wanted a brief flash with a shape you could catch, so the agent tested the bolt against black and held the branches still while the light faded.

putting lightning over a moving field

Once the flash worked on its own, I asked the agent to layer it over moving strands. It graded the field to indigo, then lifted the midtones after the darker pass hid some of the finer lines. The kick now triggers a new bolt and the snare adds a flash at the edges.

Bass and the overall level of the track change the pace of the field. When I asked for less movement in quiet passages, the first adjustment looked like dropped frames. The agent raised the minimum speed and smoothed out the changes. Around 13 seconds into the video, you can see the bolt fade while the strands keep moving underneath.
